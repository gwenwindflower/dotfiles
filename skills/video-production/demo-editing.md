# Short demo editing

Rules for product and feature demos under about 30 seconds. Apply them in Cap's timeline or Descript's.

- **Length:** a teaser runs 12 seconds at most. Shave the tail rather than speeding up the middle.
- **Cut on action:** cut on the click and resume on the loaded page. Trim page loads, spinners, and blur-ups from shot tails, and frame-check every cut point.
- **Camera cuts are content cuts:** each zoom or 3D perspective change lands on new content (a new section, page, or scroll target), never mid-idle.
- **Aim at content:** point zooms at the landmark (the hero, the clicked control, the destination header) and typing shots at the text-entry point, not the field's center.
- **One motion system:** use either 2D zooms or 3D camera moves for emphasis, never both at once.
- **Typing and terminal showcases:** use one continuous clip with a gentle tilt and a slow push-in so text stays legible. Live terminal output is free motion; dwell about 3 seconds on it.
- **QA the export:** pull a frame per beat and check that each reads, that nothing from the desktop shows, and that fps is smooth. A browser-window capture fills the frame more than a full-screen one, so back zooms off if shots read too tight.
- **Music:** only when the user wants it. Match the mood to the brand and fade it in and out.
