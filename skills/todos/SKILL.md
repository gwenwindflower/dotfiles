# Todos

Todos are personal: groceries, chores, errands, learning and writing goals, and personal mirrors of assigned work. Each one is a reminder in Apple Reminders or a task checkbox in a vault note, tracked by the Obsidian Tasks plugin. Issues, the work and project tracking in Linear or GitHub, belong to `project-management`, even when a todo mirrors one.

| Job | Doc |
| --- | --- |
| Add, find, complete, reschedule, or move a reminder | [rem](rem.md) |
| Sync, review, or plan across Obsidian Tasks and Reminders | [sync](sync.md), then [reconciliation](reconciliation.md) and [recipes](recipes.md) |

Writing or editing task lines in a note goes through `notesmd-cli`; the `obsidian` skill covers the syntax and the app-backed CLI.
